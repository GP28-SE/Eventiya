Empathy Map

Understanding our users — what they say, think, do, and feel

💬
Says
What the user expresses out loud
"I want to find events near me easily."
"Booking tickets is always so complicated."
"I wish I could see all event details in one place."
"I want to share events with my friends quickly."
"Payment should be fast and secure."
🧠
Thinks
What's on the user's mind (unsaid)
Will my data be safe on this platform?
Am I getting the best price for this ticket?
What if the event gets cancelled last minute?
Is this event actually worth my time?
I hope the organiser is credible and reliable.
⚡
Does
Observable actions and behaviours
Browses multiple platforms before deciding
Reads reviews and ratings of events/organisers
Screenshots event info to share with friends
Abandons checkout if the process is too long
Uses the calendar to avoid event clashes
❤️
Feels
Emotional states and reactions
Excited when discovering a relevant event
Frustrated by unclear ticket pricing or hidden fees
Anxious about missing out on popular events (FOMO)
Relieved when booking confirmation arrives instantly
Distrustful of platforms with a poor UI or slow load
